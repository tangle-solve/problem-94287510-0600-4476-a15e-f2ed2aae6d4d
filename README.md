### CLIM-000119: Prospective control of geothermal induced seismicity
- **Subfield:** Low-carbon electricity generation
- **Open:** Forecast damaging-earthquake probabilities early enough to adjust injection, including delayed events after shut-in. Validate calibration prospectively at unseen sites.
- **Status:** Adaptive traffic-light models and retrospective forecasts provide partial results; transferable prospective control remains unestablished.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Protects communities and prevents project-ending seismicity.
- **Key works:** Mignan et al. (2017), [“Induced seismicity closed-form traffic light system for actuarial decision-making during deep fluid injections”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-13585-9).
